    Where the data comes from

    NSE stocks and indices
    Prices, delivery, P/E, P/B, dividend yield, market cap, filings and board meetings come from the National Stock Exchange's own daily reports.
    Companies only on BSE
    Prices come from the Bombay Stock Exchange's own daily price file.
    IPOs
    NSE's public issue records and SEBI's lists of offer documents. Listing gains are worked out from official prices.
    SENSEX, S&P 500, NASDAQ and Dow
    Yahoo Finance, because no official source for these is usable here. Each of them says so beside its pivot.
    Company financials
    Yahoo Finance's annual figures for NIFTY 500 companies, labelled where they appear.
    News
    Google News, kept only from established publishers such as Reuters, Bloomberg, The Economic Times, Mint, Moneycontrol, Business Standard and CNBC.

    New prices and news arrive every evening, India time, once the exchanges publish their reports. For information only, not investment advice. Check figures against the exchange before you trade or apply.
